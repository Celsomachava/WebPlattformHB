🧹 Fix Service Worker Issues

Service Worker Issues Detected

The service worker is trying to fetch from wrong port (3001 instead of 3002)

This causes network errors and caching problems.

Manual Steps:

  1. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
  2. Go to Application tab
  3. Click "Service Workers" in left menu
  4. Click "Unregister" for any service workers
  5. Go to Storage tab
  6. Click "Clear storage" button
  7. Refresh the page (F5)
